## Corrections

1. **C1 "top-4 cities"**: the Mastercard report does not rank cities. Bangkok is named with Cancun, Hanoi and Dhaka as examples of higher-fraud destinations. Keep the 48% figure, but call it "48% of reported fraud cases in Bangkok", not 48% of tourists. Report date: about 15 May 2025.
2. **C2 ฿1.2bn**: only one source supports it (Chiang Rai Times), and that source has signs of low reliability. No official or second source was found. Remove it, or mark it "reported by Chiang Rai Times, unverified".
3. **C3 dates**: the **US$20–90** range comes from the Immigration Bureau in **March 2026** (Bangkok Post, 30 Mar 2026), not October 2025. The 17 Oct 2025 MOTS warning gives no amount. US$10 is from May 2025. New, citable figure: **at least 10% of foreign arrivals used fake TDAC sites** (Immigration, Mar 2026).
4. **C4**: the figure is correct, but it was stated by Minister Supamas, not published by OCPB. Two sources agree.
5. **C5 KhunThong**: **650k+ users is a January 2021 figure.** It is not current. No 2025–2026 evidence shows it is active or discontinued. "Free" is confirmed only as of 2021. PromptPay was not confirmed in the opened source; K PLUS and any-bank payment were.
6. **C6 Splitwise**: the daily cap is confirmed by the official Pro page, but the "3/day" number is anecdotal (2023–2025). Write "free users have a daily expense cap (reported 3–4/day)".
7. **C7 ETDA**: update to **2025 = 39,112 complaints; online shopping 36.40% (14,238)**. The older 2024 figures (35,358 / 42.56%) are superseded.
8. **C8, C9**: no reliable figure. Drop the numeric claims, or cite them only as blog estimates or individual cases.
